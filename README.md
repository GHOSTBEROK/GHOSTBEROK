## About

I'm **GHOSTBEROK**, a technology developer and researcher working where cybersecurity, software engineering, and artificial intelligence meet. I want to understand how digital systems are built, how they fail, and how to make them stronger.

- **Secure.** I study how systems behave under pressure, research vulnerabilities and security technologies, and turn what I learn into practical security solutions.
- **Build.** Web, mobile, and desktop applications, plus independent tools, scripts, and frameworks.
- **Automate.** I explore AI and intelligent automation as a force multiplier for engineering and security work.
- **Research.** I experiment with emerging technologies through hands-on R&D, then shape what works into useful, scalable IT solutions.

This profile keeps projects and experiments apart. [Featured projects](#featured-projects) lists real work with its status, and the [research lab](#the-ghostberok-research-lab) labels every experiment by maturity.
